Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy covers Vice Funny and its posting tool, Vice Funny Publisher ("the app").

What the app does

The app uploads videos made by Vice Funny to Vice Funny's own social media accounts. Only the Vice Funny account owner connects an account to it.

Information we collect

When the Vice Funny owner connects an account, the app receives an access token and basic account details (such as the account ID and username) from the platform. It may also read the view counts of our own posts. We do not collect information about anyone else, and we do not use cookies or trackers on this site.

How we use it

Only to upload our videos and to see how our own posts perform.

Sharing

We do not sell or share any information. Tokens are stored privately on our own computer.

Deleting data

Access can be removed at any time from the platform's app settings. To ask about or delete any data, email vicefunny123@gmail.com and we will delete it within 30 days.

Changes
